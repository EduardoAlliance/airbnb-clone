Welcome to Whispering Pines, a masterfully designed retreat where the wild heart of the Arizona desert meets the refined comfort of a modern timber cabin. Nestled on five private acres of rugged terrain, this sanctuary offers panoramic views of Sedona's iconic red rocks and the dense canopy of the Coconino National Forest.

The cabin features hand-hewn cedar beams, custom ironwork, and floor-to-ceiling glass walls that invite the great outdoors inside. Whether you're sipping locally roasted coffee on the wraparound deck or unwinding by the massive stone hearth, every detail is crafted to evoke a sense of heritage and tranquility.
