Curated Experiences

Beyond the cabin

Every stay includes access to handcrafted experiences that deepen your connection to the land.

🌲

Guided Forest Bathing

Slow, mindful walks through old-growth stands led by certified forest therapy guides. Rediscover the art of noticing.

🔥

Fire & Supper

An evening meal cooked over an open flame by local foragers. Wild mushrooms, cedar-planked fish, and stories by firelight.

🌌

Dark Sky Nights

Our cabins sit in certified dark-sky zones. Telescopes provided, no light pollution for miles. The Milky Way, uninterrupted.

🪵

Timbercraft Workshop

Learn traditional joinery and carving from master woodworkers. Take home something you built with your own hands.
